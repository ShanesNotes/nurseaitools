# Directory

Visitor mode: operate.

Audience: a nurse at the station, under cool fluorescent light, checking a tool before using it. Job: filter the index and read the RN reality check. Action: search, narrow, open a tool. Proof: the authored reality-check fields. Constraints: keep every tool fact, the filter meanings, the form APIs, and the canonical origin. No em dashes. No invented facts. The hero sentence "Curated by a critical care RN." is gone. The About page keeps the curator.

## Direction contract

THESIS: This is a clinical index a nurse can scan, drawn as a 1971 futures manual rather than a startup directory. It refuses the neon synthwave template and the identical icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool daylight paper `#e7eef2`, ink `#14202b`, a full-bleed field blue `#123655` masthead with ivory `#f4f1e6` wordmark. Copper `#8c4a32` is only the status-plate rule. Amber `#7a4e00` on `#f8edd4` means Unknown or not-for-patient-info. Teal `#0c5c4c` on `#e4f3ee` means Mentions HIPAA. Wordmark and plate labels are Syncopate. Body is Source Sans 3. Texture is a 1px security tint in the masthead only. Controls stay ordinary inputs and buttons, with a single chamfer, a 2px focus ring, and no glow.

STORY: The nurse sees what the directory is, filters it, and trusts a three-bay plate that says Built for, HIPAA status, and Bedside fit in the words the data already uses.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width blue masthead with the wordmark and Directory, Submit a tool, About. Under it, on paper, the sentence that most clinical AI is built for physicians, then a full-width search field. Category controls and the two filters sit on one row. The index begins immediately: name, what it does, and the three-bay plate. The count is a quiet line, not a hero number. The newsletter sits after the index.

FORM: Owner-pinned retro futurist, applied as a 1971 clinical futures manual. Not a concept-seed roll. Seed key is none, because the brief pin beats the roll. Signature move: the three-bay status plate. Motion: the copper rule draws once across the plate when a filter settles, 180ms ease-out, already-visible by default, removed under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
